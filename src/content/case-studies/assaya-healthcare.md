## Context

Assaya needed to keep track of COVID marker machines deployed across many
locations, in real time, for teams who needed to act fast on what they saw.

## The problem

Device status, location, and access were scattered. Operators lacked a single,
trustworthy view of what was where, and who could do what.

## My role

I designed the end-to-end experience for the tracking platform across web.

## What I did

- Designed real-time geo-tracking dashboards with live data visualisation and map
  views.
- Built access control plus user and device management into the core flows.
- Kept dense, fast-changing data legible under real operational pressure.

## Outcome

A single real-time surface for device visibility and management, giving teams the
clarity to respond quickly and confidently.
