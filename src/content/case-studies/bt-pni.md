## Context

British Telecom's PNI (Physical Network Inventory) initiative touches how a huge
telecom operator records and reasons about its physical network. The domain is
dense: heavy data, strict rules, and many specialist users who can't afford
guesswork.

## The problem

Complex telecom requirements were arriving faster than they could be shaped into
usable software. Design, engineering, and business each held a different piece of
the picture, which slowed delivery and risked inconsistent experiences.

## My role

I own end-to-end UX for the initiative — discovery and user research, interaction
design and prototyping, and developer handoff — partnering daily with product,
engineering, and business stakeholders.

## What I'm doing

- Translating complex telecom requirements into scalable, user-centred flows.
- Establishing design workflows and handoff documentation standards so design and
  engineering stay in sync.
- Applying AI-assisted research and prototyping to accelerate delivery inside a
  large enterprise environment.

## Outcome

A shared, consistent design language and handoff process that keeps a large,
cross-functional team moving in the same direction. *(Detailed screens and metrics
available on request — enterprise project.)*
