## Context

Industrial and manufacturing teams run on CRM, but generic tools rarely match how
these businesses actually quote, win, and deliver work.

## The problem

Sales and delivery data lived across disconnected modules, making the CRM harder to
use than the spreadsheets it was meant to replace.

## My role

I delivered end-to-end UX across the core CRM modules and built the prototypes used
in client demos and sales enablement.

## What I did

- Designed connected flows across Quotes, Projects, Opportunities, and the Data
  Warehouse.
- Shaped each module around the real sequence of an industrial sales motion.
- Built interactive prototypes that helped close enterprise deals.

## Outcome

A coherent CRM experience across the full sales lifecycle, plus demo-ready
prototypes that supported the sales team directly.
