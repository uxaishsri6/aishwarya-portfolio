## Context

Goodpack moves reusable transport assets through complex, global supply chains.
Knowing where things are — and trusting that data — is the whole game.

## The problem

Existing tracking and asset-visibility workflows were hard to follow, which hurt
traceability and slowed the people doing operational work every day.

## My role

I redesigned the core tracking and asset-visibility experience end to end.

## What I did

- Reworked tracking workflows around how operations teams actually move through
  their day.
- Made asset visibility clear and scannable, so status is obvious at a glance.
- Reduced friction in the highest-frequency tasks.

## Outcome

Better traceability, stronger usability, and improved operational efficiency across
the supply-chain workflows.
