## Context

Standing up a compliant cloud environment on AWS or GCP was a cliff for new teams:
pages of configuration, tribal knowledge about mandatory guardrails, and a real
chance of misconfiguring something security cared about.

## The problem

The provisioning flow assumed deep DevOps expertise. That made onboarding slow and
error-prone, and pushed the same questions back onto platform teams.

## My role

I owned the full UX lifecycle, from research through to implementation support,
across both cloud providers.

## What I did

- Simplified sprawling provisioning steps into a single guided dashboard.
- Baked in safe defaults and inline explanations of *why* each guardrail exists.
- Added a review step that surfaced risky choices before anything was created.

## Outcome

A clearer, faster provisioning experience that improved usability and task
efficiency — and earned internal recognition for the **Landing Zone V2** usability
improvements.
