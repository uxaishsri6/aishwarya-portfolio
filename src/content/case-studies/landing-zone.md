## The problem

Standing up a compliant cloud environment was a cliff for new teams: pages of Terraform, tribal knowledge about which guardrails were mandatory, and a real chance of misconfiguring something security cared about.

## What I designed

A guided flow — **Landing Zone** — that turned provisioning into a walkthrough with safe defaults baked in.

- Opinionated presets for common environment types, compliant out of the box.
- Inline explanations of *why* each guardrail exists, so teams learned as they went.
- A review step that surfaced risky choices before anything was created.

## Outcome

- Onboarding a new team dropped from days to hours.
- Fewer misconfigurations reached production.
- Platform teams spent less time answering the same setup questions.
