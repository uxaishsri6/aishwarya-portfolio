## The problem

Merchants in emerging markets were wary of payment infrastructure that tied them to a single cloud. Switching costs were high, and the onboarding experience assumed a level of technical sophistication many small merchants didn't have.

## What I designed

A payment gateway experience designed to be **cloud-agnostic** and approachable.

- Integration flows that didn't assume a particular cloud or deep DevOps skill.
- Onboarding that met small merchants where they were.
- Clear, trustworthy status and reconciliation surfaces for money in motion.

## Outcome

- Reached over 500K merchants in the first year after the redesign.
- Lowered the technical bar to getting started.
- Built merchant trust through transparent, legible money flows.
