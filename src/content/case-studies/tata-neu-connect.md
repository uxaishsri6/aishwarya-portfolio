## The problem

Marketing at the scale of Tata Neu meant dozens of disconnected tools — one for audiences, another for journeys, a third for reporting, and a long tail of spreadsheets in between. Campaign managers spent more time reconciling data across surfaces than actually running campaigns.

## What I designed

A single console — **Connect** — that unified audience building, journey design, and performance reporting into one coherent workspace.

- A shared audience model so a segment defined once could be reused everywhere.
- A visual journey builder that non-technical marketers could operate confidently.
- Reporting that lived beside the campaign, not in a separate BI tool.

## Outcome

- Consolidated 50+ scattered workflows into one surface.
- Cut the time to launch a new campaign dramatically by removing tool-switching.
- Established the design system patterns the wider marketing-ops suite now builds on.
