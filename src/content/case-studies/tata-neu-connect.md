## Context

Marketing at Tata Neu's scale meant orchestrating campaigns, audiences, and
reporting across many disconnected tools. Teams spent more time moving between
surfaces than actually running campaigns.

## The problem

There was no single place to define an audience once, build a journey, and see how
it performed. Fragmentation slowed execution and made data-driven decisions hard.

## My role

I led the complete end-to-end UX for the campaign and segment management system —
research, information architecture, interaction design, and handoff.

## What I did

- Designed a shared audience/segment model so a segment defined once could be
  reused across campaigns.
- Built a journey-and-campaign builder that non-technical marketers could operate
  confidently.
- Brought reporting beside the campaign instead of in a separate BI tool.

## Outcome

A unified console for data-driven marketing execution, audience targeting, and
performance tracking at enterprise scale. The patterns fed a white-label design
system that **cut design-to-development turnaround by ~40%** across the team.
