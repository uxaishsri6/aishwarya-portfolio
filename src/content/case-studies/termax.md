## The problem

Generic CRMs assume a tidy, digital-first sales motion. An automotive manufacturer's sales floor is messier: leads arrive by phone and walk-in, dealers negotiate in person, and contracts move through approvals that live half on paper.

The team had a CRM, but no one used it the way it was designed — so the data was always stale.

## What I designed

A CRM shaped around the *actual* shop-floor workflow rather than an idealised one.

- Fast lead capture that matched how reps really took down details.
- A pipeline view that mirrored the dealer negotiation stages people already used.
- Contract handling that acknowledged the paper reality instead of fighting it.

## Outcome

- Average task completion time dropped by roughly 40 percent.
- Adoption rose because the tool finally matched the work.
- Cleaner data downstream, because logging stopped being a chore.
