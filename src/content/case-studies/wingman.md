## The problem

Swipe-based dating optimises for volume, not connection. The mechanics reward snap judgements on photos and punish the slower, more honest work of actually getting to know someone.

I wanted to explore a different question: what if the app rewarded *depth* of conversation instead of speed of swiping?

## What I designed

A self-directed concept — **Wingman** — built around conversation quality.

- Matches unlocked more of a profile as a conversation went deeper, not as you swiped faster.
- Prompts that invited real answers instead of one-word openers.
- Gentle friction on the endless-swipe loop.

## Outcome

- A tested prototype run with 12 people across two rounds.
- Clear signal that the depth mechanic changed how people talked — for the better in most sessions.
- A body of work I still use to think about incentive design in consumer apps.
